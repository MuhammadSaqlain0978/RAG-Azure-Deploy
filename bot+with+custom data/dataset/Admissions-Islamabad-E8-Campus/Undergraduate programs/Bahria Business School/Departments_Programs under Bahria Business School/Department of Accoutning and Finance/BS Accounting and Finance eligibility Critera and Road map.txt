BS Accounting and Finance – Program Structure
General Information

Duration: 4 Years (8 Semesters)

Eligibility: Intermediate or equivalent with minimum 50% marks (any discipline).

Internship: 6 weeks mandatory after 4th semester.

Electives: All electives carry 3 Credit Hours (CH).

Specialization: Students must choose elective courses of Finance from BBA, MBA, or BS (Accounting & Finance) lists.
