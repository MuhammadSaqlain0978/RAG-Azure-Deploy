MS in Finance – Bahria University
Admission Eligibility

Applicants must meet the eligibility requirements set forth by Bahria University.

Candidates should confirm their eligibility before applying.

Annual system: Eligibility based on percentage marks.

Semester system: Eligibility based on CGPA out of 4.00.

If both CGPA and percentage are shown, CGPA will be considered.

Required qualification:

16 years of business/relevant education.

Students with 16 years of non-business education must complete 30 credit hours of deficiency courses before becoming eligible.
