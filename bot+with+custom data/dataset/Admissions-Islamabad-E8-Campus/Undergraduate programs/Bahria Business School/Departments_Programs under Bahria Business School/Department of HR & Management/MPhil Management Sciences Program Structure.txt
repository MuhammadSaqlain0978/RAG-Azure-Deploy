M.Phil in Management Sciences
Important Notes

Applicants must meet the eligibility requirements set by Bahria University.

Candidates are advised to confirm eligibility before applying.

For annual system, eligibility is based on percentage.

For semester system, eligibility is based on CGPA out of 4.00.

If result is given in both CGPA and percentage, CGPA will be considered.

Eligibility Criteria

16 years of education with Masters/Bachelors/equivalent degree from an HEC-recognized university in the relevant discipline/field.

Minimum CGPA 2.00/4.0 or 50% marks (where CGPA is not given).

Must pass one of the following:

GAT (General) with minimum 50% marks, OR

GRE (General) with minimum 50% marks, OR

BU Admission Test (minimum 50% marks).

Test results must be submitted at the time of application.

HEC attestation required for local degrees (on the back of original degrees).

HEC equivalency certificate required for foreign degrees.
