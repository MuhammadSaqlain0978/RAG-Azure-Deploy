MS Management Sciences
Important Notes

Applicants must meet the eligibility requirements set by Bahria University.

Candidates are advised to confirm eligibility before applying.

For annual system, eligibility is based on percentage.

For semester system, eligibility is based on CGPA out of 4.00.

If result is given in both CGPA and percentage, CGPA will be considered.

Program Details

Duration: 1.5 Years

Campuses & Timings:

Karachi Campus – Evening Classes

Islamabad Campus – Weekend Classes

Eligibility Criteria

16 years of education from HEC-recognized universities/institutes.

Background: Business / Management Sciences.

Minimum 50% marks (Annual System) OR CGPA 2.00/4.00 (Semester System).

Must pass Bahria University Admission Test OR NTS-GAT (General) with minimum 50 marks (valid within 2 years).

HEC policies (as amended from time to time) shall apply.
