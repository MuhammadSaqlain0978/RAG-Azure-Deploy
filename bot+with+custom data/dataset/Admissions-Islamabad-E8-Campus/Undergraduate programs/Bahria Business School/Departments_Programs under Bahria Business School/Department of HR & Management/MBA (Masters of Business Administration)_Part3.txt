Program Objectives (POs) – MBA

PO1: Alumni can integrate their business education knowledge with the latest industrial practices and manage dynamic challenges at national and international levels.

PO2: Alumni can exhibit advanced critical thinking, leadership, adaptability, and lifelong learning in a complex business environment.

PO3: Alumni can recognize and practice high-level ethics and social responsibility within business and society.

Program Learning Outcomes (PLOs) – MBA

PLO1: Graduates will apply advanced knowledge in business management, solving complex challenges while committing to continuous learning.

PLO2: Graduates will understand social, legal, and economic environments related to business.

PLO3: Graduates will utilize analytics tools and quantitative methods to solve business problems.

PLO4: Graduates will demonstrate advanced critical thinking and apply optimization techniques for strategic decision-making.

PLO5: Graduates will assume strategic and leadership responsibilities, with strong team-leading skills in large, complex organizations.

PLO6: Graduates will be ethically aware and able to address workplace and societal dilemmas responsibly.

PLO7: Graduates will embrace lifelong learning, adaptability, and agility in evolving industries.
