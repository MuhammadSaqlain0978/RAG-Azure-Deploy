PhD Management Sciences
Admission Requirements

18 years of MS/MPhil or equivalent education in relevant discipline from an HEC-recognized university.

Minimum CGPA: 3.00/4.00 (Semester System) OR 60% marks (Annual System).

Must pass NTS-GAT (General), GRE (General), or BU Admission Test with at least 60% marks.

An Initial Research Proposal/Statement of Purpose is required at admission.

Exception to 18-year rule only with explicit HEC authorization letter.
