Master of Business Administration (MBA)
Important Notes

Applicants must meet the eligibility requirements set by Bahria University.

Candidates should confirm their eligibility before applying.

For annual system, eligibility is based on percentage.

For semester system, eligibility is based on CGPA out of 4.00.

If result is shown both in CGPA and percentage, CGPA will be considered.

Eligibility Criteria

16 years of education with:

Minimum 50% marks (Annual System), OR

Minimum CGPA 2.00/4.00 (Semester System).
