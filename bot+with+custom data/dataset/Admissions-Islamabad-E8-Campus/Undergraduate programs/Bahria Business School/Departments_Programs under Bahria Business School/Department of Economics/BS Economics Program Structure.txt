BS Economics – Program Information
Admission Eligibility

Applicants must meet Bahria University’s eligibility requirements.

For annual system: eligibility based on percentage marks.

For semester system: eligibility based on CGPA (out of 4.0).

If both CGPA and percentage are shown, CGPA will be considered.

BS Economics (4 Years)

Intermediate or equivalent with minimum 50% marks in any discipline.
