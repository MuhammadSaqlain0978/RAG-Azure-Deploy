PhD Economics
Eligibility Criteria

18 years of education (MS/MPhil or equivalent degree) in relevant discipline from an HEC-recognized university.

Minimum CGPA: 3.00/4.00 (Semester System) OR 60% marks (Annual System).

Test Requirement: NTS-GAT (General) or GRE (General) with at least 60% marks, OR BU Admission Test (minimum 60% marks).

Initial Research Proposal / Statement of Purpose required at the time of admission.
